## Live recipe records

The app can fetch recipe records at runtime from [TheMealDB](https://www.themealdb.com/). Those records, images, and any publisher/video links belong to their respective owners and are subject to TheMealDB's terms and the original publishers' rights. The app links to original sources rather than republishing full publisher pages.
